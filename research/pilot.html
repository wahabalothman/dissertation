Pilot: claim-tracking demonstration

Six weeks // 14 Sept – 25 Oct 2026 // feasibility, not findings

PLACEHOLDER. Drafted with AI assistance and not yet written or verified by me. To be rewritten before anything here is cited or shown.

PurposeTest whether the measurement system is workable
Cohort window8–21 July 2024 (entry)
Follow-up28 days per claim, to 18 Aug 2024
Cash budget~$125 departmental pool / ~$445 with paid pretest
Effort20–25 hrs/week including learning
StatusNot started

The pilot cannot establish an adoption effect, separate the three causal routes statistically, or estimate an electorate treatment effect. It tests whether claims can be enumerated, matched, and followed reliably enough to justify building the full cohort.

01 // Frozen scope

Scope is fixed before any search for successful claims. The window is a calendar-based stress test of measurement, not a random sample of 2024.

Decision Fixed rule
Fringe frame Retrievable r/conspiracy submissions dated 8–21 July UTC; up to 50 IDs/day by fixed hash; max 700
Eligible text Title and body screened for an allegation of covert coordination plus a specific action; missing bodies marked unavailable, never “no allegation”
Unit Claim ID = actors, secret coordinated action, target, purpose, time qualifiers; unknowns stored explicitly
Cohort Enumerate all claims in the 700 posts; deduplicate before viewing elite matches; follow all if ≤60, else 60 by second fixed hash
Lookback 24 June to first sampled appearance, for prior occurrence and prior elite endorsement
Follow-up 28 days from entry; later occurrences retained separately
Intermediaries 52 Tenet records 8 July – 18 Aug, plus available weekday Anderson Cooper 360 broadcasts
Elites Sitting members in the Congressional Record, plus sitting officeholders and major-party tickets quoted directly in the selected material

Fallback window is 17–30 June 2024, chosen only if the July access gate fails, documented before enumeration. The window is never changed because few July claims were adopted.

The 60-claim cap is a workload ceiling, not a target. Twenty well-defined claims beat sixty ambiguous topics; a low yield is retained and reported.

03 // Method boundaries

Use an LLM to propose structured extractions and sentence embeddings to retrieve candidate matches; decide same-allegation and endorsement questions by hand. Do not fine-tune, build agent systems, fit Hawkes processes, or train aligned embeddings for this demonstration. A held-out benchmark decides which affordable model is adequate; there is no universal best model.

Population prevalence is not estimated here. Online posting, source popularity, elite rhetoric, political acceptability, and belief stay distinct variables.

04 // Weekly gates

Week Work Gate
1 Access checks on all five sources; freeze codebook v1 All five retrievable, or fallback window invoked
2 Enumerate claims from the sampled frame; second coder on a subsample Coders agree on claim identity at an acceptable rate
3 Build retrieval; match claims into intermediary corpus Candidate matches recoverable; precision/recall reported
4 Follow claims into the elite corpus; count endorsement events Enough events to describe, or chapter narrows to cases
5 Survey pretest: do respondents separate acceptance from belief Items load separately
6 Validation, error report, committee presentation Demonstration stands on its own

Prospectus writing is reserved for 26–31 October.
